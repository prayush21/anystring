# Motivation: founder's words (verbatim)

Spoken by Prayush, kept as written for use in the demo and pitch. Not edited.

> For the readme or the project description, I don't know where we should add this, but yeah, regarding the motivation of the project and the problem that we are trying to solve with this is basically the following. Every year, every month, every week, every weekend, do you know how many friends of yours are waiting to learn guitar? You know, try their hands on it. They'll see a video or a reel or a TikTok online and would want to play a guitar. And how many of them actually end up at playing guitar? What's a key bottleneck in getting people to create and feel their own music? And the core idea here is the friction between seeing a reel and trying to get a feel of how it is to make music, play music for people around, for yourself. And this is what we are here to solve for. And yes, it can be as simple as opening your laptop and just take the nearest racket, a tennis, a badminton racket, or even a spatula you can play some strings on, and just start by turning on the camera on your laptop. So this is what we have made here. And yeah, you can not just play guitar, you can also have this version help you with understanding the chords. It can also help you with learning a song on a guitar, because that is what we want to do. That is what we want to get started with and end up doing as well. And once you get the hang of it, once you get the hang of the strumming, that's when you know what your next step would be. We don't want to replace guitars. We just want to get you to a guitar faster. And that's what we are here for.
