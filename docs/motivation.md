# Motivation

*Edited for grammar and structure from the founder's spoken words, which are kept
unedited in [motivation-transcript.md](motivation-transcript.md).*

## The problem

Every week, every weekend, think of how many of your friends are waiting to learn
guitar. They see a video, a reel or a TikTok, and they want to try it. How many of them
actually end up playing?

The key bottleneck in getting people to create and feel their own music is the friction
between seeing a reel and getting a feel for what it is like to play: for yourself, and
for the people around you.

## The idea

Make that first step as easy as opening your laptop. Take the nearest racket, whether
it is a tennis racket, a badminton racket or even a spatula, turn on the laptop's
camera, and strum its strings.

That is what we have built.

## What you can do with it

- **Play:** strum a racket like a guitar, with nothing attached to it.
- **Understand chords:** learn how chords work and how they change.
- **Learn a song:** follow a song from start to finish. This is where we want to start,
  and where we want to end up.

## Where it leads

Once you get the hang of strumming, you know what your next step is.

We do not want to replace guitars. We want to get you to a guitar faster.
